For Pixelation:
1. Go to PC_Renderer.asset
2. Add Fullscreen Pass Renderer Feauture
3. Add pixelation shader material

For Colour Grading:
1. Make LUT texture(s)
2. Add Global Volume to Scene
3. Select Renderer (SampleSceneProfile)
4. Add LUT to Color Lookup Texture
